## 2. D-02 — V1 effective boundary (APPROVED)

**Approval basis:** owner statement of 2026-10-07 that D-02 is approved, and owner confirmation the same day that the approval covers the **complete** decision text below, not only `V1_EFFECTIVE_AT`. D-02 is not pending.

**D-02 does NOT approve** (owner confirmation, 2026-10-07):

- monthly 25/50 accumulation (the accumulation period remains PENDING);
- V1 implementation;
- schema deployment;
- migrations;
- unrestricted administrative reclassification. Classification is immutable; the only exception in clause 3 is narrow (documented, expressly approved by the owner, before validation) and grants no general reclassification power.

```
V1_EFFECTIVE_AT = 2026-11-01 00:00:00 America/Los_Angeles
                = 2026-11-01 07:00:00 UTC        (PDT, UTC-7)
```

D-02 decision text, as submitted for approval:

1. `V1_EFFECTIVE_AT` is `2026-11-01 00:00:00 America/Los_Angeles`, equal to `2026-11-01 07:00:00 UTC`. It is stored as an absolute UTC literal and cannot be modified once reached.
2. A connection is `LEGACY` if its `sale_date` is earlier than `V1_EFFECTIVE_AT` and `IZZY-COMM-V1` if equal or later. `sale_date` is the server-generated order creation time (`izzy_orders.created_at`); text date fields, `install_date`, `activation_date` and `validation_date` do not determine classification.
3. Classification is stamped when the order is created, in the database, with no default value, and is immutable. No process may reclassify a connection. An exceptional case may be resolved only by a documented administrative correction approved expressly by the owner, before the connection is validated.
4. A `LEGACY` connection keeps legacy rules, rates, rank and reserve even if activated or validated after `V1_EFFECTIVE_AT`. It does not enter the 25/50 pool, does not generate V1 leadership, and creates no V1 snapshot, V1 ledger entry or V1 period membership.
5. A V1 connection freezes its economic snapshot at `validation_date` (D-04). `validation_date` is not the classification criterion. The commission table version is determined by `sale_date`. The V1 period of the connection is the `America/Los_Angeles` month of its `validation_date`.
6. Chargebacks, adjustments and corrections on a `LEGACY` connection are always governed by the legacy policy, on any date. They create no V1 negative balance and no adjustment in V1 periods. Chargebacks and adjustments on a V1 connection follow V1 rules and post in the current open period.
7. Period boundaries are computed with real time-zone conversion to `America/Los_Angeles`, not a fixed offset. Period `2026-11` runs from `2026-11-01 07:00:00 UTC` to `2026-12-01 08:00:00 UTC` (exclusive), 721 hours.
8. Initial V1 ranks and hierarchies are loaded with `effective_at` equal to `V1_EFFECTIVE_AT`.
9. This decision does not modify frozen documents 01–05 and is recorded separately.

**Corrections applied:** the period-start example in `06-PERIOD-CLOSE-DESIGN.md` §2 (`2026-11-01 00:00:00-08` / `08:00 UTC`) was incorrect and has been corrected on 2026-10-07 to `2026-11-01 00:00:00-07` / `07:00 UTC`; 1 November 2026 at 00:00 Pacific is still PDT (UTC-7). Clause 7 prevails. The `DEFAULT 'LEGACY'` in `02-V1-EFFECTIVE-BOUNDARY.md` §5 is replaced by clause 3.

## 2A. D-03 — Grandfathering discriminator (APPROVED 2026-10-07)

**Owner confirmation:** D-03 is formally approved.

```
sale_date = izzy_orders.created_at   (generated by the server)
```

No other date determines LEGACY/V1 classification. `validation_date` is not the LEGACY/V1 discriminator; it remains the D-04 economic snapshot anchor.

| Condition | Classification |
|---|---|
| `created_at < V1_EFFECTIVE_AT` | `LEGACY` |
| `created_at >= V1_EFFECTIVE_AT` | `IZZY-COMM-V1` |

The classification is immutable and there is no silent `DEFAULT 'LEGACY'`. LEGACY remains economically isolated from V1.

## 5. Active, inactive and unsupervised users (APPROVED RULES)

1. **Active users:** receive an initial V1 event according to the mapping in §3.
2. **Inactive users:** receive **no** automatic initial event. Their V1 rank is assigned explicitly when they are reactivated.
3. **Users without `manager_user_id` / `sponsor_user_id`:** no supervisor is assigned by inference and no hierarchy is guessed. Their V1 supervisor remains `NULL` until an explicit owner decision.

## 6. Initial V1 events — conceptual design (APPROVED)

`izzy_v1_rank_events`:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `user_id` | `bigint` (matches `izzy_portal_users.id`) |
| `rank_code` | `training`, `associate` or `supervisor` |
| `effective_at` | `2026-11-01 07:00:00 UTC` |
| `reason` | `initial_v1_onboarding` |
| `approved_by` | authorized owner/admin (`bigint`) |

- Append-only. A rank change is a new event that closes the previous one, with explicit `effective_at` and approval.
- **No fallback** to `izzy_portal_users.compensation_role`. If a V1 rank is missing, the engine fails closed.
- `izzy_v1_hierarchy_events` uses `bigint` identifiers and a `NULL` `supervisor_id` where §5.3 applies.
